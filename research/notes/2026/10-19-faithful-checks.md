# Faithful check of the end-of-page region on the integrated head (task faithful-checks)

Status: MEASURED (Chromium counts, CI), verdict computed by Lean (`Fw.Faithful`, run by `fwcore faithful.verdict`).
Educational study of the public fastwork.com homepage, not affiliated with Fastwork.

## Question and unit

Does the rewrite R of the integrated head (commit 4ebd67b7, the ports) stay inside the PL5 bands of the original A in the
last scroll zones `y04000`, `y04500`, `tail`, for every count type of the frozen class table? Bands: rAF callbacks,
layers, compositor/main split exact; style, layout passes, elements within max(2, 5 %); paint and raster within
max(2, 10 %); sheet mutations 0; stable types add the margin 2t (PLAN.md PL5). A is the frozen
`results/baselines/chromium/<p>/original.baseline.json`; the class table is `classes.frozen.json`.

## Method

- Lean: `Fw.Faithful.Band` (family allowlist, `inBand`, `status`), `Fw.Faithful.Zone` (cell, zone and region verdict),
  `Fw.Faithful.Wire` (op `faithful.verdict`), goldens `packages/contracts/golden/faithful/`, measured theorems
  `Fw.Faithful.Measured` (generated by `packages/measure-chromium/src/faithful.mjs lean`, proved by `decide`).
- CI: workflow `chromium-counts`, target `both`, 3 runs. swipe-v1 on branch
  `ci/faithful-checks` (run 39130842058), swipe-touch-v1 on `ci/faithful-checks-touch` (run 39131232365).
- Control: the original of the same run (A') against the frozen A with the same verdict code.

## Results

| profile | arm | y04000 | y04500 | tail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| swipe-v1 | A' vs A (control) | pass | pass | pass |
| swipe-v1 | R vs A | fail | fail (absent in R) | fail |
| swipe-touch-v1 | R vs A | no data | no data | no data |

- swipe-v1 R, zone shapes: A scrolls 500 px in `y04000` and 136 px in `y04500` (page end at 4636 px); R scrolls 396 px in
  `y04000` and has no `y04500`, so its page ends at 4396 px, 240 px earlier. Tail: 30 frames, 0 px in both.
- `y04000`: 23 of 44 judged cells outside the band (tally pass 21, fail 23, skipped 10, unbanded 11); `tail`: 13 of 38.
  Names and numbers: `results/faithful/swipe-v1.verdict.json` (`verdict.zones[].offBand`).
- The control passes all three zones with zero failing cells: the harness, the class table and the verdict code
  reproduce the original within its own bands, so the failures belong to R, not to measurement noise.
- swipe-touch-v1: the three rewrite shards stopped with "touch input did not scroll the page after 40 moves"
  (`src/run.mjs:277`), the three original shards of the same run succeeded. `results/faithful/swipe-touch-v1.finding.json`.

## Reading

- The `y04000` and `tail` counts of R are not comparable to A as long as the pages end at different scroll heights; a
  shorter page moves content across zone boundaries. The shape failure (px or frames differ) is the first finding; the
  out-of-band cells are listed as measured, not explained.
- Findings only: nothing was patched. Causes (missing section or shorter footer in R; touch not scrolling R) are not
  diagnosed here (INFERRED candidates only, not claimed).

## Limits

Chromium only; one CI run per arm (3 shards); profiles swipe-v1 and swipe-touch-v1 (the second without R data); no second
gate run. Stable types use the PLAN margin 2t as a proxy, not a zone-level noise proof. Forced layouts have no count type
of their own. R is judged by the median of its 3 shards. WebKit and pixels are out of scope. The verdict is raw counts,
work, not milliseconds.
