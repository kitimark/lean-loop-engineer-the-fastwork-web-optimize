# Why the hero exit and the end-of-page morph lag in Safari

This note uses source and docs only. Nothing here is measured on a device; webkit-lag-probe measures.

## Answer

- **Hero exit (region A).** JS writes `--exit-opacity` and `--exit-scatter` on `.hero` once per scroll frame. WebKit never runs a custom property in the UI process. Each frame then costs a restyle of the readers, a layout (the blur outsets change), a **full repaint of the `.copy` and `.cue` layers** (a Layout diff marks the layer for full repaint), a new CA filter radius, and a render-server re-blur at up to 18px.
  - Predicted dominant cost, Mac: the per-frame repaint plus re-blur of the copy group, and the copy trailing the async-scrolled sticky hero by one or more commits (INFERRED).
  - Predicted dominant cost, iPhone (≤600px): `.copy` and the deck have `filter: none`. What remains is main-thread rendering-update latency per delivered scroll position plus the cue repaint and blur (INFERRED).
- **End of page (region B).** A 680 ms rAF tween writes `--morph`, which drives `top`, `width` and `height` of the fixed nav. Each frame then lays out from the view root and fully repaints every resized backed layer: the nav, the panel and the 5 (desktop) or 3 (phone) image tracks. At the end state that is about 4.6 + 4.6 + 8.5 ≈ 17.7 Mpx per frame on a 2x Mac and at most about 2.7 + 2.7 + 4.8 ≈ 10.2 Mpx on a 3x iPhone (areas INFERRED from CSS, formulas in the per-layer table; the iPhone tracks are tiled, so their share is an upper bound). Four blurs change radius every frame: the panel `filter`, the platform `filter`, the earn button `filter` (first 20%) and the nav `backdrop-filter`. The earn button's CSS transitions on `max-width`, `padding-inline` and `opacity` restart on every `--morph` change, so it keeps being laid out for about 0.68 s after `--morph` passes 0.2 (per-transition effects below; INFERRED).
  - Predicted dominant cost: the resize repaints (raster in the GPU process), plus view-root layout; render-server blur and backdrop work come second (INFERRED).
  - Per transition: at morph start about 80 countdown faces and the 5 or 3 tracks regain `will-change` and become new layers, and the column images get their first decodes (INFERRED from CSS and the decode path below).
  - Mac input: a **mouse wheel** scrolls synchronously on the main thread while the non-passive `wheel` listener is armed, so each long frame shows as scroll stutter. A **trackpad** gesture turns async after its first event (≤ 50 ms wait) unless that first event is default-prevented (CONFIRMED). A trackpad gesture that starts before the armed band cannot be stopped later, so `pin()` fights the async scroll on Mac as it does on iPhone (CONFIRMED path; visible effect INFERRED).
- **Images.** No WebKit-side cache of a scaled image exists: each repaint of a track re-draws each column image scaled from its full decoded bitmap (CONFIRMED). The 21 column images are not all 1282px: 5 role PNGs are 1282 × 1282 or 1282 × 1281, and the other 16 are 341-960 × 270-1042 (MEASURED, `sips` on the snapshot files). Decoded, they take 61.66 MB on desktop (21 images) and 30.07 MB on a phone (13 painted) (MEASURED; formula in the Images section). At the end state the source-to-drawn ratio runs from 0.38 to 2.24 on a 2x Mac, where 9 of 21 images are upscaled, and from 0.25 to 1.91 on a 3x iPhone (INFERRED, because the track width comes from CSS). The cards (region A) only recomposite, so their images are not redrawn (CONFIRMED path). The panel blur and the nav backdrop act on rasterized layers, not on the images (INFERRED, closed source).
- **Is Safari "unoptimized" here?** Partly. CSS and WAAPI animations of `filter`, `opacity` and `transform` get the CA animation path; custom properties never do. Threaded time-based animations are a preview preference, off by default. [Bug 228312](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=228312) (blur cost) is open, with a fix reverted in 2026-01. The site routes both regions onto the slow path: JS-written custom properties, a layout-driven morph and a radius that changes every frame (INFERRED).

- **Backdrop.** The `.backdrop` paints nothing of its own, so it has no backing (INFERRED).

**Per-transition effects.**
- **Mount** (morph.mount, `rootMargin: 200% 0px`): the panel subtree enters the DOM with the scene `paused` (no `will-change`), opacity 0 and blur 12px. That costs one style, layout and image fetch. A zero-opacity layer is skipped in paint, so the column images are likely not decoded before the morph (`RenderLayer.cpp:6144-6149`; INFERRED).
- **Morph start**: `data-start-selling-scene` leaves `paused`. The tracks regain `will-change: transform` and their 16s animation, and the countdown digit faces regain `will-change: transform`: about 8 digits × 10 faces ≈ 80 new layers, plus ancestor clipping layers for `.digit { overflow: hidden }`. Each new layer is created, gets a first paint with async image decode, and is committed (morph.columns/morph.module.css:9-12, morph.countdown/morph.module.css:13-17, hero.countdown/hero.module.css:71-72, hero.countdown/index.tsx:30-55; Q8; INFERRED counts).
- **First morph frames**: once a column image has painted and is visible, the next repaint decodes it synchronously. A full-size synchronous decode on the main thread happens only to an image with two properties. First, its natural size exceeds the size it was drawn at on first paint. Second, its `cover`-drawn size grows as the column widens, which means it is width-dominated: the drawn size is `natural · max(dW/nW, dH/nH)`, and dH is a fixed height. An image that is height-dominated at the end-state width is height-dominated at every narrower width, so its drawn size never changes. By that rule no phone image qualifies, because all 13 are height-dominated at 124 CSS px. On the Mac, 5 of 21 are width-dominated at the end state: work-a, architect and work-e (downscaled) and service-kitchen and avatar-cat (upscaled at the end, possibly downscaled at first paint). Which of them qualify is image-audit's (pending). (Q8; CONFIRMED path; the set, the count and the timing are INFERRED.)
- **Earn button** (nav.module.css:167-185, no `.is-morphing` override; the phone block at :281-284 changes only the values): `max-width` and `padding-inline` have 0.68 s transitions, and `opacity` has a 0.32 s transition after a 0.12 s delay (:179-182). Each frame that `--morph` changes their computed values, WebKit cancels the running transition and starts a new one with the full duration from the current value (`WC/style/Styleable.cpp:742-758`). So the button's layout runs until about 0.68 s after `--morph` passes 0.2 (`--earn-out` saturates, :168-170), which can outlast the 680 ms tween. It does not stop at the first 20%. While the opacity transition runs, it likely composites the button (`RenderLayerCompositor.cpp:3842-3853`); otherwise its blur is painted (`RenderLayerFilters.cpp:160-235`). Status: retarget path CONFIRMED; duration and compositing INFERRED.

**Predicted dominant cost (INFERRED).** First, the resize repaints (about 17.7 Mpx per frame at the end on Mac, at most about 10.2 on iPhone) and view-root layout. Second, at morph start, the layer creation and the image decodes. Third, the render-server blur and backdrop work. On Mac with a mouse wheel, any long frame on the approach shows as scroll stutter. Chrome hides more of this because its compositor scrolls and rasters off the main thread (engine contrast, not measured).

A blurred composited layer is blurred from its rasterized backing in the render server; the blur does not re-sample the images inside it (INFERRED, closed source). ## WebKit cost terms `Fw.Engine.WebKit` lacks

1. **Repaint on radius change:** a blur radius change is a Layout diff that fully repaints the filtered layer's content each frame, on top of the CA re-blur (Q2b).
2. **CA filter raster:** filtered area × radius in the render server, each frame the radius or the subtree changes.
3. **Backdrop sampling:** backdrop area × radius when the content behind it or the rect changes; the `cMaxTotalBackdropFilterArea` cap; the rounded-rect clip update.
4. **Painted filter:** a non-composited filter costs (dirty rect + outsets) × radius (`RenderLayerFilters.cpp:160-235`). The earn button is one when its `opacity` transition is not running; while that transition runs it is likely composited, which makes its blur a CA filter (`RenderLayerCompositor.cpp:3842-3853`). Which path applies is INFERRED.
5. **Tiled vs untiled backing:** above 2048 (Mac) or 1280/1024 (iOS) CSS px × page scale a layer is tiled and paints only its coverage; a crossing swaps the layer type (Q6).
6. **Paint split:** web-process recording vs GPU-process raster (Q1b).
7. **Image draw:** a scaled draw from the decoded frame per repaint (natural px × repaints), the decode mode per paint (async first paint, sync later, a full-size sync decode when the drawn size grows), and decoded memory (Q8).
8. **Layer creation:** layers created when `will-change` returns at morph start, with first paint and commit.
9. **Overlap churn** from filter outsets in overlap bounds (`RenderLayer.cpp:5638-5644`, `RenderLayerInlines.h:38`, `RenderLayerCompositor.cpp:2594-2612`).
10. **Input path by type:** trackpad gesture (≤ 50 ms wait, then async unless the first event is prevented; later `preventDefault` ignored; the gesture and its momentum keep the steps latched at its first event, so a gesture that begins with no listener gets no DOM dispatch: `ScrollingTree.cpp:136, 144-148`, `ScrollingTreeLatchingController.cpp:59-73, 91-128`, `PlatformWheelEvent.h:221-229`), mouse wheel (synchronous per event), iOS touch (preventable until the pan starts), and the commits by which main-thread visuals trail the async scroll.
11. **Cadence:** one commit in flight, a 60 fps preference (Prefs.yaml:6505-6520), and the Low Power Mode and thermal throttle to 30 fps (`WC/platform/graphics/AnimationFrameRate.h:43, 50-57`; `WC/page/Page.cpp:544-549, 2809-2817`).
12. **Device scale** in paint area (2x Mac, 3x iPhone).
13. **Policy trigger:** `Policy` exists, but the memory condition that switches it to Conservative (`RenderLayerCompositor.cpp:705-722`) does not.

## Limits

- The render server, CoreAnimation and CoreGraphics are closed source, so every GPU and resample cost is INFERRED.
- Layer reasons for the panel, veil, center, cue and platform are read from CSS and source, not observed; a WKTR layer dump would confirm them.
- Areas and scale ratios are computed for the end state of a 1440 × 900 Mac and a 390 × 844 iPhone viewport (`100vh` taken as 844).
- The note has two measurements: the StyleDifference CI run (Q2b) and `sips` on the snapshot image files. It has no device or CI timing, and per-image drawn sizes, decode counts and waste ranking belong to image-audit.
