17 `tb-verdict` golden rows: every cell is the golden one except an `overlap` that became `undecided`, and the verdict
recomputed from the printed cells is the golden verdict.

## The floor: 3 (INFERRED from the cells below, MEASURED counts)

Rule: raise the floor only if the callers' cells that carry a decision already hold it, so no decision cell is lost.

| Caller | Cells that decide | Smallest side | Cells below 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| toolbar report (`flagRow`, pooled over jobs) | 5 flags x 3 columns | 5 usable passes (default beside unthrottle: 4) | none; the pinned unit asked 3 passes per arm |
| morph-end report (`brickRow`, 18 jobs of runs 40158609202, 40167641467, 40193540518) | 5 arms | 3 faithful first stretches (flip) | none |
| touch-lock pooled over the two `touch-lock-windows` jobs | 3 arms | 5 accepted windows | none |
| touch-lock per job (4 runs) | 3 arms per run | 0 to 3 accepted windows | runs 40060600880, 40065281372 (arms with no accepted window), 40071802991 (base 2), 40074062290 (lock 2) |

The per-job touch-lock cells are not the decision (the across-job verdict is task `sim-touch-verdict-jobs`); each printed
`overlap`, never `lower` or `higher`, so no claim is withdrawn. Two samples a side are separated by chance in 1 of 3 of
the arrangements when nothing differs (`2 / C(4,2)`); three a side in 1 of 10 (`2 / C(6,3)`); that is the arithmetic reason
for 3, INFERRED to carry over to these counts.
