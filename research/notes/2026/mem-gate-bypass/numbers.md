# mem-gate-bypass: numbers from samples.jsonl

Generated by `node research/notes/2026/mem-gate-bypass/reduce.mjs <samples.jsonl>`; do not edit by hand. Rules:

- Lean process: argv0 basename exactly `lean`, `lake`, `propslint` or `fwcore`; parenthesised (exiting) rows such as `(lean)` are excluded.
- Lean RSS: sum of ps RSS (KB) over the Lean processes of one sample. MiB = KB/1024 rounded to the nearest integer (sums are
  rounded once, after summing); GiB = KB/1024/1024 to two decimals.
- Tree: a process and all its descendants by ppid in the same sample.
- VM counters are the sampler's `vm_stat` pages x page size / 2^20, labelled MB as the sampler does; GB = MB/1024 to one decimal.
- Time: sample `ts` at UTC+07:00 (the sampler host's local time).
- Burst: a sample whose Lean RSS is at least 4096 MiB; its "before" sample is the previous index.
- Heavy-lock process: args contain `scripts/heavy-lock.mjs run`. Inner check.sh: its child with args `sh scripts/check.sh`.
  Outer root: walk up from the heavy-lock process while the parent's argv0 basename is `zsh` (the Bash tool's shell).
- Jobs of a check.sh tree: its processes with argv0 basename `lean` or `propslint`.
- fwcore-build tree: rooted at a process with args `sh scripts/fwcore-build.sh` or `sh ../scripts/fwcore-build.sh`.
- Location class from the cwd: `land-wt/<n>` (below `.claude/land-wt/<n>`), `agent worktree` (below `.claude/worktrees/`),
  else `main checkout`; only the class is printed.
- Agent build: a `lake build` process whose every ancestor in the sample has argv0 basename `zsh`, `timeout` or `claude`.
- Axiom check: tree rooted at a process whose args contain `axiom-check.sh`. Lake serve: tree rooted at `lake serve`.
- Serena Lean: the process with argv0 basename `uv` whose args contain `serena start-mcp-server` and `--project lean`; its parent must have argv0
  basename `claude` (the reducer exits non-zero otherwise).
- Output holds pids, sample indices, counts and sizes only: no cwd and no args.
