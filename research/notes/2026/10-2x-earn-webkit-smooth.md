# Earn button on WebKit: the `nav:earn-logical` variant (task earn-webkit-smooth)

## Answer

- **MEASURED (macOS CI, 2 valid runs) and proved in Lean:** listing `padding-inline-start, padding-inline-end` in place of `padding-inline` in
  both earn transition rules makes WebKit start the side-padding transition at every hero-pill flip, entry and exit. The padding no longer
  jumps from 24 px (16 px at 640 px and below) to 0 in the flip frame; it eases over about 520 to 540 ms, as Chromium does.
  The earn width and the language button then move smoothly: at 1280 px the largest one-frame jump of the language button falls from
  48 px to 18 px on exit and 33 px on entry (the remaining jump is the one dropped frame at the flip plus the ease itself).
- **Chromium is unchanged:** same started set and same duration and delay for every property (Lean `variant_chromium_set`,
  `variant_chromium_timing`). On the measured runs Chromium with the variant eases like Chromium without it.
- **Look-change class L, "engine parity":** on WebKit the look changes (the faithful rule snaps); Chromium and settled frames do not.
  Ledger row n=73 `nav.earn.logicalPadding`, review pending: the user's Safari (macOS) and iPhone check. The iPhone is INFERRED from
  shared WebKit source.
- **Not delivered here:** the breakpoint gate with the variant needs a `fw` input, which only a dispatch carries (see Limits).

## The variant

`apps/site/src/parts/nav/index.tsx` puts the class `is-earn-logical` on the header when the variant is `earn-logical`. Two rules at the
end of `nav.module.css` (five classes of specificity, above the faithful earn rules, which have four) repeat the faithful lists with
the one entry changed: `padding-inline` becomes `padding-inline-start` and `padding-inline-end`, same `.68s` and
`cubic-bezier(.33,0,.2,1)`. The hero-pill rule keeps opacity `.2s` without a delay, the other keeps `.32s` after `.12s`. Without the flag
nothing changes: the class is absent and the rules never match.

Reading: faithful WebKit and Safari change the padding by its full value in one frame (24 px wide, 16 px narrow) and "settle" at 0 ms;
with the variant the largest padding step is 1.5 to 5.5 px, the same size as Chromium's (frames on the runner are 17 ms in WebKit and

390 px: padding 16, 15.9, 15.4, 13.5 ... 4 px (faithful: 16, then 0). The opacity fade is the same length in all arms.
Chromium variant against Chromium faithful differ by run noise only (it ran 30 fps).

- Playwright WebKit on macOS stands in for Safari at 390 px; Safari itself (safaridriver) agrees at 1280 px. No iPhone was measured.
- The breakpoint gate: push runs of the unflagged default page passed (run 40100783656, Chromium and Playwright WebKit on Linux), but the gate
  with the variant needs `fw=nav:earn-logical`, an input only a dispatch has. Lead command:
  `gh workflow run breakpoint-gate.yml --ref ci/earn-webkit-smooth -f engines=chromium -f fw=nav:earn-logical`.

- Felt smoothness belongs to the user.

## Gate comparison of the logical variant (continue round)

Breakpoint gate run 40106804693 failed only on the five `transition-*` lists of nav role earn: the faithful rule lists `padding-inline`, the variant its two logical longhands, so the computed lists differ in length (`0.68s, 0.2s, 0.68s` against four entries). CONFIRMED by the merge summary of that run. Fix: `Fw.Gate.TransitionList.normalize` expands a listed shorthand to its longhands (repeating the entry's other four values; css-transitions-1 §3), proved idempotent (`normalize_idem`), the identity on lists without a listed shorthand (`normalize_of_not_hasShort`), and the two earn lists normalize equal (`earn_equal`). The gate normalizes both arms before comparing (`packages/gate-breakpoints/src/transition-list.mjs`, golden `gate/transition-list.jsonl`). Limit: the shorthand table is the nine listed names; the comma split of the computed strings is a TS stand-in.

## Findings: runs 40106804693 and 40111361865

- 40106804693 (breakpoint gate, variant on): FAILED on the raw transition lists (`padding-inline` against its logical longhands). Fixed by the Lean normalization (`Fw.Gate.TransitionList`, golden used by the gate).
- 40111361865: normalization worked, every non-reduced-motion state passed. FAILED with 41 chromium readings, only nav role earn, only reduced-motion states: the original computes `none`, `0s`, `ease`, `0s`, `normal` there; the variant computed its own four-entry list. Cause: the variant's earn rules (five class selectors) outrank the original's reduced-motion `transition: none` on `.btn.earn` (two) and the same weight issue held for `.is-earn-still` (four). Not a race: specificity.
- Fix: Lean `Fw.Parts.NavEarnWebkit.reduced_started_empty` and `reduced_computed_faithful` (empty started set on both engines and directions; the computed list equals the faithful `none` list); the variant rules now sit inside the existing `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` block and exclude `.is-earn-still`, so both conditions fall through to the original rules. Golden rows `reduced` (16), checked by `earn.webkit.golden.test.ts`. Dispatch: `gh workflow run breakpoint-gate.yml --ref ci/earn-webkit-smooth -f engines=both -f fw=nav:earn-logical`.
- Moving the rules shifted the trajectory table: `lean/Fw/Gate/Trajectory.lean` rows (variant rules now lines 235 and 242, spec 6, `is-earn-still` off; reduce rows 304, 311, 312), its golden `packages/contracts/golden/gate/trajectory.jsonl`, `packages/gate-breakpoints/src/trajectory/reduce.mjs` and `test/trajectory.test.mjs` (a no-preference block always applies in the probe).
