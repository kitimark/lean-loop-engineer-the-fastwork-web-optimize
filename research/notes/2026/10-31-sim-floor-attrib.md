# sim-floor-attrib: what schedules the second style recalc per frame on the iOS Simulator, and what is the recording's own floor

Task `sim-floor-attrib`, follow-up of `10-31-sim-end-floor.md` section 5. On the CI Simulator with every animation paused, base read 100 recalcs per 100
frames with 0 scheduled, and v2-proved (v2p), v2p minus `hero.exit:threaded` (minusT) and v2p minus `engine.scroll:unthrottle` (minusU) read 200 with 360
scheduled per 360 frames. Both minus arms still hold a scroll-driven animation. This note adds three arms that each differ from a named comparator in one
variable, and traces the source path. No flag or preset change.

## 1. Run

- MEASURED: run 41145066709 (workflow webkit-sim, commit `1815c4260cd3270239370a2372fa17199fb5be33`), 3 separate CI jobs r1, r2, r3, one round each,
  runtime `com.apple.CoreSimulator.SimRuntime.iOS-27-0` (`results/sim-floor-attrib/r1/attrib.txt:2`). Seven arms per job; the arm order is rotated by 2 per
  job (r1 starts at base, r2 at minusT, r3 at blank; `results/sim-floor-attrib/r1/job{1,2,3}.json`, `rounds[0].order`). Window 360 `requestAnimationFrame`
  callbacks, three stages per load (end idle, back at the end, all animations paused), rate = recalcs per 100 frames, band 10, as in sim-end-floor.
- Arms (each added arm differs from its comparator in one variable; Lean `Fw.Device.SimFloorAttrib`):
  - `blank`: a static page with an empty body, same build, same harness and recording (part `content`, against base).
  - `minusBoth`: v2p minus `hero.exit:threaded` and minus `engine.scroll:unthrottle`; no scroll-driven animation is left (part `platform:faded-pause`
    against base, `engine.scroll` against minusT, `hero.exit` against minusU).
  - `v2pCancelScroll`: v2p; at the paused stage every animation whose timeline is a ScrollTimeline or ViewTimeline is cancelled (`anim.cancel()`), the others are
    paused (part `scrollAnimations`, against v2p, paused stage only).
- Every window is readable: `seen` is 360 in 55 of 63 windows and 362 to 378 in the other 8, all in the end stage (rows.txt, `seen`).
- Job r2 needed 3 reconnects (minusT once, minusU twice, each `Runtime.evaluate timed out after 120000 ms`, `results/sim-floor-attrib/r1/job2-log.txt:4, :7, :11`); the
  reported windows are from the fresh load after the reconnect. Jobs r1 and r3 had none.

## 4. Source path (WebKit@862f17d)

One animation on a scroll or view timeline costs one scheduled style recalc per frame, paused or finished or not, unless it is a skipped-content animation or the frame-rate aligner skips its frame (`AnimationTimelinesController.cpp:150`, `:171`).

| # | step | file:line@862f17d | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Animations step of every rendering update calls `Document::updateAnimationsAndSendEvents` | `Source/WebCore/page/Page.cpp:2307-2309` | CONFIRMED (read) |
| 2 | The timelines controller visits every timeline whose `documentWillUpdateAnimationsAndSendEvents()` is Yes: a ScrollTimeline when its source element is connected, a ViewTimeline when its subject is | `Source/WebCore/animation/AnimationTimelinesController.cpp:104-257`; `ScrollTimeline.cpp:291-298`; `ViewTimeline.cpp:444-450` | CONFIRMED |
| 3 | It ticks every `relevantAnimations()` entry of such a timeline: no play-state filter and no accelerated-state filter (its only skips are skipped content, `:150`, and the frame-rate aligner, `:171`), so paused and finished animations tick | `AnimationTimelinesController.cpp:149-177`; `AnimationTimeline.h:59` | CONFIRMED |
| 4 | `WebAnimation::tick` calls `m_effect->animationDidTick()` unless effect invalidation is suspended; the progress-based auto-align returns early for a paused animation with a hold time | `WebAnimation.cpp:1558-1582`, `:1502-1527` | CONFIRMED |
| 5 | `KeyframeEffect::animationDidTick` calls `invalidate()` unconditionally; `invalidateElement` calls `Element::invalidateStyleForAnimation` unless a style recalc is running | `KeyframeEffect.cpp:2129-2131`, `:1731-1735`, `:127-135` | CONFIRMED |
| 6 | `invalidateStyleForAnimation` calls `Node::invalidateStyle(AnimationInvalid)`, which marks the ancestors only when the node was valid, then `Document::scheduleStyleRecalc` | `Element.cpp:2763-2767`; `Node.cpp:1006-1024`, `:972-985` | CONFIRMED |
| 7 | `scheduleStyleRecalc` starts the 0 s timer and records `didScheduleStyleRecalculation` (the Timeline `ScheduleStyleRecalculation` record) only if the timer is not active: one record per frame however many animations tick | `Document.cpp:2783-2795` | CONFIRMED |
| 8 | After the rAF callbacks `layoutIfNeeded()` flushes the dirty style: the `RecalculateStyles` record | `Page.cpp:2329-2337` | CONFIRMED |
| 9 | A DocumentTimeline ticks only while `m_animationResolutionScheduled`, so a paused document animation does not recalc | `DocumentTimeline.cpp:190-197` | CONFIRMED |
| 10 | `cancel()` makes the animation idle, hence not relevant; the next tick removes it from the timeline, and `ScrollTimeline::updateCurrentTimeIfStale` returns early when it holds no animation: no tick, no invalidation | `WebAnimation.cpp:837-892`; `AnimationTimelinesController.cpp:179-180`, `:236-243`; `AnimationTimeline.cpp:82-93`; `ScrollTimeline.cpp:309` | CONFIRMED |
| 11 | `hasProgressBasedScrollDrivenAnimation` only feeds `hasMainThreadScrollDrivenAnimations` (`WebPageCocoa.mm:2357`); it schedules nothing | `AnimationTimelinesController.cpp:267-279` | CONFIRMED |

- Steps 1 to 8 are read from the source and agree with the measurement: one `ScheduleStyleRecalculation` record and one extra `RecalculateStyles` record per frame
  (360 and 360 extra per 360 frames, `rows.txt:16-18`) in every arm that holds a scroll or view animation, none in the arms that hold none (`rows.txt:15, 20, 21`),
  and none after `cancel()` (step 10, `rows.txt:21` and the census `:42-43`). Step 3 has no accelerated-state filter, so the threaded pref does not remove the
  tick; that the pref was ON in the run is INFERRED (section 5).
- INFERRED, not read in the source: that the one unscheduled recalc per frame of the paused base (`rows.txt:15`) has a page cause. The blank arm excludes the
  recording; which page part causes it is not traced.

## 5. Preference at run time

- The harness now records `defaults read` of the Safari and global domains, filtered for `Threaded` and `ScrollDriven`, in `meta.prefOverrides`: no override in jobs r1 and
  r2 (`com.apple.mobilesafari` domain not found, global empty; `job1.json`, `job2.json`), and an empty read failure in r3 (`job3.json`). That is no evidence of an
  override, and not a read of the effective value: no documented read of the effective `ThreadedScrollDrivenAnimationsEnabled` value exists from the host.
  The value stays INFERRED: ON by source default (`Source/WTF/Scripts/Preferences/UnifiedWebPreferences.yaml:8518-8531`; `KeyframeEffect.cpp:3253-3259`).
- The result does not depend on the value: step 3 ticks every relevant animation of the timeline whether or not it has an accelerated representation.

## 6. Candidate optimization (spec only, a follow-up; no site change)

Claim to test: park the finished scroll and view animations at the end of the page and pause the off-screen document animations together, with the same look.

- The 14 scroll and view animations are finished at the end of the page (census `rows.txt:25`: `view:finished` 13, `scroll:finished` 1). The parks already exist
  as flags: `hero.exit:threaded-park` and `engine.scroll:unthrottle-park` (`10-30-scrolldriven-park.md` sections 2 to 3, look identity proved in Lean).
- Prediction from this run (INFERRED): parks alone leave the live end-of-page rate at 200 per 100, because minusBoth reads 200 at the end stage (section 3). A cut
  needs the 5 running document animations (the cue float and the 4 platform sweeps, `rows.txt:37`) to stop too while they are off screen; both together would reach the
  paused-stage floor of 100, scheduled 0.
- Arms (each one variable from its comparator, the same harness): v2p + both parks; v2p + both parks + a pause of the off-screen cue float and platform sweeps; compared
  with v2p. The latter needs a look-identity proof per animation (the platform sweep is visible while the section is on screen).
- Out of reach of this candidate: the remaining 1 recalc per frame of the paused base.
