## 2. Park variants (class E candidates)

- `hero.exit:threaded-park`: while the end of the exit range is more than one viewport above the viewport top, the 13 finished animations are
  cancelled and each target holds the keyframe end value as a static style; restored by the observer rule or the scroll rule.
- `engine.scroll:unthrottle-park`: cancels the flag animation after 134 ms without scroll, restores at touch start or scroll.
- Look identity (held value = fill value per target and property): `Fw.Parts.ScrollPark.fill_is_anim_end`, `finished_shows_to`, `held_eq_shown`,

- Restore (no stale frame): `io_no_stale`, `io_restores` (needs a per-frame jump of at most one viewport), `scroll_no_stale`, `scroll_restores`;
  fw-ut: `ut_scroll_unparks`, `ut_touch_unparks`, `ut_park_only_still`, `restored_unthrottles`.

Sources: `results/scroll-park/r1/verdict.txt` (`results/scroll-park/r1/verdict.txt:79`: `both parks: mixed (jobs none | none | cut)`), `results/scroll-park/r2/rates.txt`,
`results/scroll-park/r2/parkdiag.txt`. One park alone never cuts (`none`, 3 of 3 jobs, both arms), as the `shared` split predicts. Both parks cut the
end-of-page floor from 100 to 0-1 per 100 in 1 of 3 jobs per run; in the other jobs the hero animations stay scroll-driven (13 of 14; the fw-ut
animation is the one parked) so the floor stays. Mid-page: no cut (`partial` in j3 of run 1, base floor remains).

Mechanism of the intermittent engagement (INFERRED, not tested): the hero park trigger is an IntersectionObserver on a one-pixel sentinel. An
observer reports when the sentinel's intersection state changes. A scroll that jumps from the top of the page to the end moves the sentinel from
below the viewport to above it without ever intersecting, so no callback arrives and the page is never parked. The Lean `ioParked` assumes an
observation every frame; the TS glue does not give that. Fix candidate (follow-up, needs a Lean statement of the delivery rule): a trigger that
reports on `scrollY` crossing the boundary regardless of jumps.

## 4. Class and gate

- Look and restore proofs hold (above). The breakpoint gate for both park flags on one page (run 41054732597): chromium pass (7056 readings,
  7049 pass, 0 fail, 7 excused); playwright-webkit fail only in `hero.orbit` and `hero.deck` cells (147 fail readings); parts `hero.exit` and
  `engine.scroll` pass on both engines. A default-config baseline run is the comparison (see handoff for its id and result).
- Class E claim: `engine.scroll:unthrottle-park` keeps class E (proofs hold, parks every time in the trace); `hero.exit:threaded-park` keeps
  class E for the look, but does not deliver the cut reliably; recorded as a negative finding, not shipped to any preset.
